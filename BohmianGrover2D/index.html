WHAT EACH STEP DOES

  1. Prepare

    Spread one basis state across the entire grid, with equal probabilities.

  2. Mark

    The oracle rotates the marked amplitude by π. Its probability stays the same.

  3. Amplify

    Undo preparation, flip the initial-state phase, then prepare again. Together, these three gates increase the marked probability.

About the gates and colors

On 2×2 and 4×4 grids, H is the tensor-product qubit Hadamard.

The reference gate flips the chosen initial amplitude. H Sᵢ H† is the Grover reflection up to an overall minus sign, which leaves probabilities unchanged.

With a different initial state, preparation still gives equal probabilities, but phases may differ. Colors show the amplitudes’ phases; a common phase shift of every column has no measurable effect.

Between gates, the display follows the same smooth unitary interpolation as the non-free demonstration. The gate endpoints are the ordinary logical circuit.
